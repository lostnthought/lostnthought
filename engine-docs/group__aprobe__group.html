Generates a synthetic IR tail for a given AudioProbe based on its RT60.

\begin{align} E_b &= \mathrm{Envelope} \\ f &= \mathrm{Fade\ Start} \\ s &= \mathrm{IR\ Samples} \\ B &= \mathrm{Band\ Count} \\ H_b &= \mathrm{Biquad\ Filter} \\ d_b &= \mathrm{Decay} \\ y &= \mathrm{Output\ Samples} \\ & \textbf{for } n \in{}[f,s) \textbf{ do}\\ & \quad \omega[n] = \gamma \cdot \mathcal{U}(-1, 1) \\ & \quad \text{where } \gamma = \frac{1}{0.577350268} \\ & \quad y_b[n] = b_{0,b} \cdot (w[n] E_b[n]) + b_{1,b} \cdot x_b[n-1] + b_{2,b} \cdot x_b[n-2] - a_{1,b} \cdot y_b[n-1] - a_{2,b} \cdot y_b[n-2] \\ & \quad E_b[n] = E_b[n-1] \cdot d_b \\ & \quad y[n] = \sum_{b=0}^{B-1}H_b(\omega[n] \cdot E_b[n]) \\ \end{align}

Parameters
